Days to max for Clash of Clans

    How Max Planner works

    Paste the data export from Clash of Clans and the planner works out every upgrade left to max your Town Hall, or any higher hall you pick: buildings, traps, walls, heroes, research and pets. It then schedules them across your builders, Laboratory and Pet House, trying dozens of orders and keeping the one that finishes first.

    What it takes into account

    • Upgrades already running in your export, and every Town Hall, Laboratory, Hero Hall and Pet House requirement.
    • Merged buildings from Town Hall 16, and the Eagle Artillery joining the Town Hall at 17.
    • Your Builder's Apprentice and Lab Assistant, at the levels in your export.
    • The Gold Pass boost over the season, event discounts such as Hammer Jam, and Clan War League if you keep heroes ready for it.
    • Magic items you have and expect each month: potions when every worker is busy, books on long upgrades, hammers on long waits.
    • The hours you are offline: nothing starts while you sleep.

    What it leaves out for now

    • Walls are counted, with their cost, but not scheduled: they take no builder time.
    • Supercharges, guardians, crafted defenses, Town Hall weapons, hero equipment and the Builder Base.
    • Loot: the plan assumes you can afford each upgrade when its turn comes.

    Your data

    Everything runs in your browser. Your export is never uploaded. Villages and settings you plan are kept in this browser only, so you can come back to them; remove a village from the Start page at any time.

    Game data and credits

    Costs, times and requirements come from ClashKing's static data, taken on 16 November 2026 for Town Hall 19. That data is licensed GPL-3.0, and so is the game data file built from it. Thanks to the ClashKing team. Rounding of discounted times follows ClashClock's write-up.
